I worked on a dedicated embedded kit for field-data collection. The
hardware combined a Raspberry Pi, an STM32 microcontroller, a resistive
touchscreen, and multiple peripheral ports, with a camera and GPS receiver as
the primary peripherals.

Several Python services ran on the Raspberry Pi. A platform connector received
planned routes and returned telemetry, while background/watchdog logic moved
prepared images into Google Cloud Storage. The Linux-side software used Flask,
SQLAlchemy, GDAL, SQLite, and EXIF-processing tooling.

The STM32 acted primarily as a camera controller. It could trigger image capture
automatically when the vehicle approached a target point or manually from the
device controls. The firmware was written in C++, with SPI used for communication
between the Raspberry Pi and the microcontroller.

This project also incorporated the validation and synchronization workflows that
had previously existed as scripts on field laptops.

## Responsibilities

- Participated in electrical-schematic design and peripheral integration.
- Configured SPI communication between Raspberry Pi and STM32.
- Developed the STM32 firmware in C++.
- Developed control scripts capable of updating controller firmware.
- Developed and integrated Linux-side Python services on the Raspberry Pi.
- Participated in data-validation and synchronization integration.
- Integrated image transfer to Google Cloud Storage.
