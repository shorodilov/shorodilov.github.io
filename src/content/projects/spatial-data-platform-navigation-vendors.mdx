A platform composed of services for collecting, analyzing, and packaging
spatial data for navigation vendors in the EU and USA.

Work focused primarily on core libraries that performed low-level data
manipulation and exposed higher-level interfaces to other platform components,
alongside GPS data parsing and POI density analysis.
