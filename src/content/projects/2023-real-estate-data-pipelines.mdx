I worked on an internal analytics service for a real-estate company.
Source data arrived as files in Google Drive, commonly CSV as far as I remember,
and was transformed into queryable analytical datasets.

A custom Python worker handled ingestion from Google Drive and initial
serialization before loading data into BigQuery. Apache Beam and DataFlow were
used across the broader inputs-to-BigQuery-to-reports processing flow. I also
developed SQL-based methods for querying BigQuery and preparing report datasets.

Prepared analytical outputs were delivered back into the company's Google Drive
workflow. Google Cloud Storage may have appeared as an intermediate layer, but I
no longer remember that detail reliably enough to include it as part of the
required pipeline.

## Responsibilities

- Explored incoming customer/internal datasets and designed ETL processes.
- Developed Google Drive ingestion and serialization logic.
- Created and populated BigQuery tables for analytical processing.
- Worked with Apache Beam and DataFlow cloud-processing workflows.
- Developed SQL queries and methods for preparing analytical reports.
- Tested workflows and deployed data pipelines to the platform.
