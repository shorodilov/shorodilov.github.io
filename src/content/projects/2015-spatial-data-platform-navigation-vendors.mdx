I worked on a large platform that grew naturally from several
smaller spatial-data projects. Multiple navigation customers had different
delivery requirements, but the operational flow was similar: customers supplied
points of interest, routes were prepared with Google mapping and distance
services, field teams collected the required data, and the results were
normalized into customer-specific delivery packages.

The platform was built as a distributed set of services running primarily on
GCP. It provided order placement and tracking, field-team management, background
route preparation, segmentation of routes to fit working-time limits, field-data
ingestion, customer-specific serialization, delivery packaging, and a large
notification system serving customers, managers, field teams, and administrators.

## Core service

I worked primarily on the Python/Falcon core service, which orchestrated data
flow between other services. Celery and RabbitMQ handled asynchronous task
distribution and durable transport of field data. Services authenticated to the
core with API keys.

I also developed a reusable Python SDK for other backend teams. It serialized
data into the format expected by the core service and exposed higher-level HTTP
methods for operations such as passing work to another service or requesting
validation.

The platform used PostgreSQL/PostGIS for spatial and operational data including
customer POIs, planned routes, and field results. SQLite/SpatiaLite provided
local storage on field devices, while MongoDB held mostly administrative data
such as orders and invoices.

Field laptops prepared pending data in chunks and submitted it through a REST
service that forwarded work to RabbitMQ. The client kept retrying while data was
pending and connectivity was available; local records were marked as processed
after server acknowledgement. The core service applied customer-independent
validation such as photo-metadata checks and GPS-track continuity checks.

The wider platform also integrated with OneDrive in the Microsoft/Azure
ecosystem and Dropbox. I periodically contributed to a Django administration
service and participated in requirements gathering and architecture planning for
both the platform and the core service.

## Responsibilities

- Developed and evolved the platform core service.
- Implemented orchestration and data exchange between distributed services.
- Developed the internal Python SDK for other backend developers.
- Worked with Celery/RabbitMQ asynchronous processing and field-data transport.
- Contributed to the Django administration service when required.
- Participated in platform requirements gathering and architecture planning.
- Supported validation, routing, delivery, and operational workflows.
