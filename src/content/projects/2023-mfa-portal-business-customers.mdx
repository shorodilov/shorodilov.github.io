During the Christmas/New Year holiday period, I briefly joined an existing business-facing
MFA/account-management product while much of the primary team was away. The
engagement was short and served largely as an introduction to an unfamiliar
codebase and technology stack.

The project used Python 3.10, Twisted, Tornado, React, and AWS. My contribution
was intentionally limited rather than ownership of a major subsystem.

## Responsibilities

- Learned and navigated the existing Twisted/Tornado application stack.
- Fixed a couple of minor application bugs.
- Added several additional parametrized test cases.
- Worked within the established product architecture and development process.
