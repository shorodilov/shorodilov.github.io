business-facing MFA/account-management product while much of the primary team

My contribution focused on targeted maintenance work in the existing codebase:
fixing minor application defects and extending parametrized test coverage.

## Responsibilities

- Worked within the existing Twisted/Tornado application stack.
- Fixed a couple of minor application bugs.
- Added several parametrized test cases.
- Followed the established product architecture and development process.
