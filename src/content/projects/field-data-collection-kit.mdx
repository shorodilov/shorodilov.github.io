An all-in-one field kit for spatial data collection teams serving navigation
projects in the EU and USA. The system handled GPS data, provided route points
to field teams, and streamed collection progress to managers.

The wider solution combined onboard software with server-side data aggregation,
distribution, coverage analysis, and a management dashboard. Communication
between field kits and server services was secured with JWT.
